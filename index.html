The five specimens

A floor price is the most optimistic number in the building: it is the cheapest thing anyone will still sell you. Everything above it is belief. Below it is nothing.

What a floor actually is

1 · A bid, not a bank

“Floor × supply” is a convenience, not a bank balance. It assumes the next ten thousand buyers pay what the next one did. In a thin book, selling even a fraction of a supply walks the floor down through every price on the way.

2 · Belief compounds upward, then doesn’t

Prices rose because prices rose — a reflexive loop funded by new arrivals. When arrivals stopped, nothing held the loop up. The bloom was fed by the same mechanism that starved it.

3 · The survivorship lie

These five are the famous ones — the winners. Thousands of collections closed at exactly zero and are not in this chart. Judging the bubble by these five understates the carnage.

Method

Data

Hand-compiled monthly floor prices for five collections, Jan 2021 → Oct 2026. Rounded, monthly, approximate — good enough to trace the shape of a bubble, not good enough to trade on. Full provenance in data/README.md.

The flower

Written twice on purpose: once in Python with Pillow (src/flower.py) to print the plates, once in canvas (site/assets/flower.js) to animate them. Same maths, so the picture and the chart can never disagree.

Reproduce
